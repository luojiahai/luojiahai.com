Cantonese · Main · About 2.5 hours

> The amounts are a reference only. Adjust them as you see fit.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Beef brisket, rib-side cut (牛坑腩) | 2000g |
| Radish | as needed |
| Chu Hou paste (柱侯酱) | 100g |
| Hoisin sauce (海鲜酱) | 20g |
| Satay sauce (沙爹酱) | 15g |
| Red fermented bean curd paste (南乳酱), or the cubes (南乳) | 15g (or 2 cubes) |
| Pixian doubanjiang (郫县豆瓣酱) | 10g |
| Oyster sauce | 8g |
| Ginger | 20g |
| Shallots | 15g |
| Garlic | 10g |
| Garlic sprouts (蒜苗) | as needed |
| Bay leaves | 4 leaves |
| Star anise | 3 pods |
| Cinnamon | half a stick |
| Cloves | 4 |
| Tsaoko (草果) | 1 |
| Xinhui dried tangerine peel (新会陈皮) | 1 piece |
| Rock sugar | 30g |
| Sesame oil | 2g |
| Ground white pepper | 3g |
| Chicken powder | 5g |
| Rice wine (米酒) | to taste |
| Maggi seasoning (美极) | to taste |

## Steps

### 1. Sear the brisket

Cut the fresh brisket into chunks and put them straight into the pan. Fry until the outside is browned, drizzling a round of rice wine over it as it fries to take away the gamey smell and add aroma. Move the browned beef into a stockpot and set it aside. Pour off the excess oil in the pan.

### 2. Fry the sauces

Heat the pan and add oil. Add the ginger, shallots and garlic and fry until golden on the outside. Then pour in the mixed sauces, drizzle a round of rice wine, and stir-fry on high heat until fragrant.

### 3. Combine in the pot

Add boiling water and stir until the sauces are evenly mixed in. Then add the rock sugar, spices, ground white pepper and chicken powder. Mix well and pour it all over the beef.

### 4. Simmer low and slow

Bring it to a boil on high heat, then turn it down to the lowest heat and simmer for 2 hours.

### 5. Prepare the radish

In the meantime, peel the radish and cut it into chunks. Bring a pot of water to the boil, add two spoonfuls of rock sugar, and put the radish in. Cook for 15 minutes, until the surface of the radish turns slightly translucent, then lift it out and set it aside.

### 6. Add the radish

When the brisket has 15 minutes left, add the radish and cook it with the brisket for another 15 minutes.

### 7. Reduce and serve

Uncover the pot, lift out the brisket and radish, and strain the broth. In a separate wok, fry the garlic sprouts until fragrant, add the brisket and radish, and pour in the strained broth. Season with a little oyster sauce and Maggi, then reduce on high heat. Once the sauce is thick, add a little sesame oil, then move everything into a hot clay pot and top with garlic sprouts. It's ready to eat.

## Notes

- Rib-side brisket has plenty of tendon and tender meat, so it holds up to long braising and the texture comes out better
- Blanching the radish in rock-sugar water first takes away its bitterness and makes it more translucent
- Serve in a clay pot after the final reduction, since it keeps the heat well
