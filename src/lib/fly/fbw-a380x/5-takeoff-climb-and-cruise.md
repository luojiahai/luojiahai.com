# 5 · Takeoff, Climb, and Cruise

Holding point to cruise level.

## Base Knowledge

If MSFS started you on the runway, the FMS and AFS still have to be set up as in
the earlier phases. Then skip to Takeoff.

> [!NOTE]
> If you come from other airliners, note that the sidestick commands the flight control
> computers, not the surfaces, and autotrim holds the pitch whenever you let go,
> autopilot or not. Autothrust never moves the thrust levers. They set the most
> power it may use, and after takeoff they stay in the CL detent. On the AFS
> control panel, push a knob for managed mode and pull it for selected; dashes in
> the SPD or HDG window mean managed. In MSFS, clicking a knob up pushes it and
> clicking it down pulls it. The flight phases run preflight, takeoff, climb,
> cruise, descent, approach, go around and done, and the flight envelope
> protections make a stall or overspeed close to impossible.

On the [FMA](controls.md#displays), check thrust **MAN TOGA**, vertical **SRS**
green with **CLB** armed in blue, and lateral **RWY** green with **NAV** armed
in blue. The autopilot is still off, so the flight director only shows the
guidance and you fly it by hand.

### Monitor Speeds and V-Speeds

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V~1~ | monitor | hand off the thrust levers at V~1~ |
| [SIDESTICK](controls.md#lateral-console) | perform the **rotation** at V~R~ | 2–3°/s for about 5 s, to 12.5° pitch |
| Positive climb | confirm | V/S positive on the PFD |
| [LANDING GEAR](controls.md#landing-gear-and-brakes) lever | set **UP** | – |
| [L/G](controls.md#landing-gear-and-brakes) indicators | check the gear **up** | and on the SD WHEEL page |
| [AP1 / AP2](controls.md#afs-control-panel) pushbutton | set as required | – |

> [!NOTE]
> V~1~ is a cyan `1` on the speed tape, V~R~ a cyan circle, V~2~ a magenta
> triangle. On a long runway V~1~ and V~R~ sit so close the marks overlap. The
> throttle hand stays on the levers until V~1~ so you can abort, and comes off
> at V~1~ so you cannot abort by accident. Count the rotation, one-one-thousand,
> and reach 12.5° at about four or five, then follow the flight director.

## Initial Climb

Thrust stays at FLX/MCT or TOGA until the thrust reduction altitude, typically
about 1500 ft above the runway and set in the FMS.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [THRUST](controls.md#thrust) levers | set **CL** | **LVR CLB** flashing on the FMA |
| [A/THR](controls.md#afs-control-panel) pushbutton | check **active** | A/THR in white on the FMA |
| [PACK 1 & 2](controls.md#air-conditioning-and-bleed) pushbuttons | set as required | – |
| [AP1 / AP2](controls.md#afs-control-panel) pushbutton | press **AP1** | AP1 in white, top right of the FMA |
| [FLAPS](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | set **1** | at **F** speed |
| [FLAPS](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | set **0** | at **S** speed |
| [NOSE](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switch | set **OFF** | – |
| [RWY TURN OFF](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switch | set **OFF** | – |
| [SPEED BRAKE](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | set **RET** | disarms the ground spoilers |
| [APU BLEED](controls.md#air-conditioning-and-bleed) pushbutton | set **OFF** | – |
| [APU MASTER SW](controls.md#auxiliary-power-unit-apu) pushbutton | set **OFF** | – |
| [TCAS](controls.md#surveillance) field | set **TA/RA** | MFD SURV page |
| [ENG ANTI ICE](controls.md#anti-ice) pushbuttons | set as required | – |
| [WING ANTI ICE](controls.md#anti-ice) pushbutton | set as required | – |

> [!NOTE]
> In CL the autothrust is active, and in normal flight you will not touch the
> thrust levers again until final approach. The aircraft climbs to the altitude
> on the AFS control panel.

> [!NOTE]
> Retract the flaps one step at a time, by the marks on the PFD speed tape. From
> **2**, retract to 1 at **F** and to 0 at **S**. From **1+F**, retract at **S**,
> with the speed trend positive.

> [!NOTE]
> The taxi and runway turn off lights switch off by themselves when the gear
> retracts. Move the switches to OFF anyway.

This is usually the moment to check in with Departure:
[ATC Communications › Departure](../shared/atc-communications.md#4--departure-and-centre-climb-and-cruise).
Without a higher clearance the autopilot levels off at the selected altitude.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ALT](controls.md#afs-control-panel) knob | dial the **cleared altitude** | – |
| [ALT](controls.md#afs-control-panel) knob | **push** for CLB, or **pull** for OP CLB | – |

> [!NOTE]
> Climb in CLB or OP CLB, not V/S. V/S guidance outranks speed guidance, so a
> rate the aircraft cannot hold makes it speed up or slow down until the speed
> limit is reached, and only then does the V/S give way.

## Climb

The climb to the cruise level usually comes in steps, each cleared by ATC. The
autopilot holds the selected or managed speed and manages the thrust.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ALT](controls.md#afs-control-panel) knob | set the **cruise flight level** as cleared | one step per clearance |
| [ENG ANTI ICE](controls.md#anti-ice) pushbuttons | set as required | – |
| [WING ANTI ICE](controls.md#anti-ice) pushbutton | set as required | – |

### At 10,000 ft

The aircraft accelerates to the climb speed on the PERF CLB page.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LAND](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switch | set **OFF** | – |
| [SEAT BELTS](controls.md#signs) sign | set as required | **OFF** once stable: good weather, no planned turns |
| [EFIS CP](controls.md#efis-control-panel) options | set as required | – |
| [MEMO](controls.md#ewd) | review | – |
| Optimal and max altitude | check | – |
| [BARO selector](controls.md#efis-control-panel) | push for **STD** | at the transition altitude, when the baro setting flashes on the PFD |
| [ISIS BARO](controls.md#isis) | set **STD** | – |

> [!NOTE]
> The transition altitude depends on the country: 18,000 ft in the U.S., 3000 ft
> in the Netherlands. Standard is 29.92 inHg or 1013 hPa.

## Cruise

Levelled off at the cruise level, at the cruise speed on the PERF CRZ page. It
is the quietest part of the flight: walk the ECAM pages, and expect frequency
changes and position check-ins.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [FMA](controls.md#displays) | check **ALT CRZ** in the second column | at the CRZ FL on PERF CRZ |
| [MEMO](controls.md#ewd) | review | – |
| [SD](controls.md#system-pages) pages | review | – |
| Flight progress | monitor | – |

Typical requests from ATC, or because of weather and traffic:

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ALT](controls.md#afs-control-panel) knob | dial the new level and **push** | on a level change |
| [HDG/TRK](controls.md#afs-control-panel) knob | dial the heading and **pull** | on a heading; push to rejoin the route |
| [SPD/MACH](controls.md#afs-control-panel) knob | **pull**, then dial the speed | when ATC assigns a speed |

| Page / field | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [F-PLN](controls.md#flight-plan) | select the waypoint, then **FROM P.POS DIR TO** | on a direct-to a waypoint in the plan |
| [DIR TO](controls.md#flight-plan) | type the waypoint | not in the plan: DIR TO button, bottom right of F-PLN |
| [DIR TO](controls.md#flight-plan) | press **INSERT DIR TO\*** | – |

Start descent planning **200–300 NM** from the destination.
