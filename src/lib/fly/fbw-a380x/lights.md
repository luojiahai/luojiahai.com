# Lights by Phase

Every light state, in one matrix. The procedure pages say *when*; this says
*what*.

`–` means no action: the previous state carries over.

## Exterior

| Light | Prep | Start and Taxi | Takeoff | Initial Climb | Above 10,000 ft | Descent 10,000 ft | Final (G/S green) | After Landing | Shutdown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [STROBE](controls.md#exterior-lighting) | **AUTO** | **ON** to cross a runway | **ON** | – | – | – | – | **AUTO**; crossing a runway **ON**, then **OFF** | **OFF** |
| [BEACON](controls.md#exterior-lighting) | OFF | **ON** | – | – | – | – | – | – | **OFF** |
| [WING](controls.md#exterior-lighting) | as required | – | – | – | – | – | – | as required | OFF if on |
| [NAV](controls.md#exterior-lighting) | **ON** | – | – | – | – | – | – | – | **OFF** |
| [RWY TURN OFF](controls.md#exterior-lighting) | as required | as required | **ON** | **OFF** | – | – | **ON** | as required; **OFF** at the gate | **OFF** if still on |
| [LAND](controls.md#exterior-lighting) | as required | **ON** to cross a runway | **ON** | – | **OFF** | **ON** | ON | **OFF**; crossing a runway **ON**, then **OFF** | – |
| [NOSE](controls.md#exterior-lighting) | as required | **TAXI** | **T.O** | **OFF** | – | – | **T.O** | **TAXI**; **OFF** at the gate | **OFF** if still on |

- **STROBE** AUTO fires the strobes once the main gear strut is no longer
  compressed, so they stay dark on the ground. Set ON for the takeoff roll and
  whenever you cross a runway. After landing the guide sets AUTO, and OFF once
  across a runway on the way in.
- **BEACON** on before pushback, off once N1 is below 5 % on all four engines.
  It tells the ground crew the engines are about to start or still running.
- **NAV** on with power, off when securing the aircraft. With NAV on and the
  aircraft on the ground, the white obstruction lights on the wing tips come on
  too.
- **LOGO** has no row: it is INOP in the A380X. On the real aircraft its AUTO
  position lights the tail logo on the ground, and in flight with the slats out.
- **RWY TURN OFF and NOSE** sit on the nose gear strut and switch off by
  themselves when the gear retracts. Move the switches to OFF anyway. NOSE at
  T.O lights the taxi and takeoff lights, at TAXI the taxi lights only.
- **RWY TURN OFF** is printed RWY TURN OFF & CAMERA. The camera lights come on
  only with the taxi cameras (ETACS), which are INOP, so the switch works the
  runway turn off lights alone.
- **LAND** is ON or OFF. Off above 10,000 ft in the climb, on again by
  10,000 ft in the descent.
- **WING** lights the wing leading edges and the engine intakes, to check for
  ice.

## Interior and Signs

| Control | Prep | Above 10,000 ft | Descent 10,000 ft | Shutdown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [SEAT BELTS](controls.md#signs) | **ON** | as required, **OFF** once stable | **ON** | **OFF** |
| [NO MOBILE](controls.md#signs) | **AUTO** | – | – | **OFF** |
| [EMER EXIT LT](controls.md#signs) | **ARM** | – | – | **OFF** |
| [INTEG LT](controls.md#cockpit-lighting) | as required | – | – | – |
| [GLARESHIELD LT](controls.md#glareshield-lighting) | as required | – | – | – |

- **SEAT BELTS** off above 10,000 ft only once stable: good weather, no planned
  turns. Back on by 10,000 ft in the descent. Its AUTO position, which the
  guide does not use, lights the signs with the engines running and the slats
  or gear extended.
- **NO MOBILE** takes the place of a NO SMOKING sign, which the A380 does not
  have; the guide's NO SMOKING at shutdown means this selector. At AUTO the
  signs come on below about 13,000 ft AGL, or over a country that bans mobile
  phones.
- **EMER EXIT LT** at ARM lights the emergency lighting if normal electrical
  power fails. At OFF, an amber OFF light shows on the selector.
- **INTEG LT** stands for the pedestal cockpit lighting knobs: INTEG LT,
  MAIN PNL FLOOD LT, PEDESTAL FLOOD LT and AMBIENT LT, each from OFF to BRT.
  **GLARESHIELD LT** is the knobs under the glareshield.
