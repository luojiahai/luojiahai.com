# 2 · Cockpit Preparation

Cold and dark at the gate to a powered, prepared cockpit.

## Pre-checks

Every switch should already be in its position before anything gets power. Set
the parking brake; the rest you are confirming, not changing.

## Initial Power Up

The batteries come first. External power then feeds the systems while the
engines are off, and the aircraft needs at least two external power units to
power the whole electrical network.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ALL BAT](controls.md#electrical) pushbuttons | set all four **AUTO** | BAT 1, ESS BAT, BAT 2, APU BAT; the guide's ON |
| [EXT 1 & 2 & 3 & 4](controls.md#electrical) pushbuttons | set **ON** in the order 2, 3, 1, 4 | – |
| [INTEG LT](controls.md#cockpit-lighting) knob | adjust as required | with the [MAIN PNL FLOOD LT](controls.md#cockpit-lighting), [PEDESTAL FLOOD LT](controls.md#cockpit-lighting), [AMBIENT LT](controls.md#cockpit-lighting) and [GLARESHIELD LT](controls.md#glareshield-lighting) knobs |

## Fire Tests and APU Startup

The fire tests prove the detection and extinguishing systems work. The APU then
supplies power and bleed air while the engines are off. The radios go on now in
case of an emergency.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [RMP 1 & 2](controls.md#radio-management) | set both **ON** | BRT/OFF knob |
| [RMP frequency](controls.md#radio-management) | set the **communication frequencies** | VHF page |
| [APU FIRE](controls.md#fire-protection) pushbutton | check **in and guarded** | – |
| [APU AGENT](controls.md#fire-protection) pushbutton | verify **OFF** | – |
| [ENG 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 FIRE](controls.md#fire-protection) pushbuttons | check all **in and guarded** | – |
| [ENG AGENT 1 & 2](controls.md#fire-protection) pushbuttons | verify all **OFF** | both agents on every engine |
| [FIRE TEST](controls.md#fire-protection) pushbutton | press | chime, warning lights and ECAM all respond |
| [APU MASTER SW](controls.md#auxiliary-power-unit-apu) pushbutton | set **ON** | – |
| [APU START](controls.md#auxiliary-power-unit-apu) pushbutton | set **ON** | the [SD APU page](controls.md#system-pages) shows the start |
| [EXT 1 & 2 & 3 & 4](controls.md#electrical) pushbuttons | set as required | keep **ON** in hot weather to cut the APU load |

> [!NOTE]
> A good fire test gives a constant repetitive chime and a flashing
> [MASTER WARN](controls.md#glareshield-side-panels) light. The ECAM shows
> `ENG 1(2)(3)(4) FIRE`, `APU FIRE` and `MLG BAY FIRE`. Every engine and APU
> fire pushbutton lights red, the agent pushbuttons show **SQUIB** and
> **DISCH**, and the fire lights on the engine master panel come on.

> [!NOTE]
> The SD APU page comes up on its own when the APU starts, and switches back to
> the previous page once the APU is running.

## Cockpit Preparation Flow

The flow starts at the bottom left of the overhead panel and works up, then
right. It carries on down the main panel to the pedestal, programs the FMS, and
finishes on the glareshield and the lateral consoles. Two pilots normally share
it; alone in the sim, you do it all.

1. Overhead panel, left to right and bottom to top: prepare.
2. Main instrument panel: prepare.
3. Pedestal: prepare.
4. MFD: see [3 · Preparing the FMS](3-preparing-the-fms.md).
5. Glareshield: set and crosscheck.
6. Lateral consoles: oxygen mask test.
7. Windows: check closed and locked.
8. PFD and ND: check.

position.

> [!NOTE]

> [!NOTE]
> Get the IFR clearance before departing, from clearance delivery or the
> frequency the airport uses. It usually gives the destination and route, the
> initial altitude, the SID or radar vectors, the departure runway, the squawk
> and the departure frequency. Read it back, or acknowledge it in the built-in
> ATC menu. The [CRAFT mnemonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRAFT_(aviation))
> helps.
