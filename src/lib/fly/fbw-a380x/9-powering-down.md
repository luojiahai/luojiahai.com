# 9 · Powering Down

At the gate, back to cold and dark.

## Parking at the Gate

Engines still running, APU **AVAIL** from the taxi in, **APU BLEED** still off.
Lights may still be in the taxi configuration: RWY TURN OFF on, NOSE at TAXI,
landing lights off.

## Disembarking Passengers and Baggage

Parking checklist complete. Real life starts most of this by itself; in the
simulator you call it in.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GROUND SERVICES](controls.md#ground) page | connect the **jetway** | flyPad, `Ctrl+0`; PAX |
| [GROUND SERVICES](controls.md#ground) page | call **baggage** | – |

> [!NOTE]
> The aircraft cannot be shut down before everyone has disembarked. For a
> turnaround, stop here and pick the preparation flow back up: the cabin crew
> handle disembarking and resetting the cabin, and once refuelled you signal
> them to board the new passengers when the cabin is ready.
