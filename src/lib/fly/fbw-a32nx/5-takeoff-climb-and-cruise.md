# 5 · Takeoff, Climb, and Cruise

Holding point to cruise level.

## Down to the Line

The BEFORE TAKEOFF checklist splits at the line: everything above it is done
before the takeoff clearance, while you wait at the holding point.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overhead panel | scan for **dark, APU off** | packs may be off if that is the procedure |
| Flight controls | **checked** | if not already done during taxi |
| Flight instruments | **checked** | – |
| [CALLS ALL](controls.md#calls) pushbutton | press to get **CABIN READY** | the cabin crew's signal, shown on the ECAM |
| [FLAPS](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | verify the **takeoff position** | matches PERF TAKE OFF |
| [TAKEOFF (FLAPS, FLEX TO TEMP, V1, VR, V2)](controls.md#performance) page | verify **V1, VR, V2** and the **FLEX** temperature | – |
| [ATC code](controls.md#atc-and-tcas) | verify the **squawk** | – |
| [ATC mode](controls.md#atc-and-tcas) selector | set **AUTO** or **ON** | – |
| [ALT RPTG](controls.md#atc-and-tcas) switch | set **ON** | – |
| [RMP frequency](controls.md#radio-management) | set the standby to **Departure** | quick swap after takeoff |
| [MEMO](controls.md#ewd) | verify **no blue** on the takeoff memo | AUTO BRK MAX, SIGNS ON, CABIN READY, SPLRS ARM, FLAPS T.O, T.O CONFIG NORMAL |
| [T.O CONFIG](controls.md#ecam-control-panel) pushbutton | press to **test** the configuration | – |
| [WX RADAR MODE](controls.md#radar) selector | set **SYS 1** | weather appears on the ND |
| [PWS](controls.md#radar) switch | verify **AUTO** | set during taxi |

> RWY TURN OFF on, LAND on and NOSE at T.O. together are what minimise the bird
> strike hazard on the roll.

## Takeoff

1. Release the [PARK BRK](controls.md#parking-brake) and hold the manual brakes down.
2. [THRUST](controls.md#thrust-and-trim) levers — advance to about **50 % N1** and
   wait for both engines to stabilise there.
3. Push the sidestick **half forward** to load the nose gear.
4. Release the brakes and set **FLX/MCT** or **TOGA**, depending on whether you
   entered a FLEX temperature and whether the runway is long enough.
5. Check the [FMA](controls.md#displays): thrust **MAN FLX**, vertical **SRS**
   green with **CLB** armed, lateral **RWY** green with **NAV** armed, both
   flight directors on, **A/THR** armed but not active.
6. At about **80 kt**, ease the forward pressure off; neutral by **100 kt**.
7. Keep the hand on the thrust levers until **V~1~**, then take it off — after
   V~1~ the takeoff is no longer abortable.
8. At **V~R~**, rotate smoothly at about **3°/s** for roughly 5 seconds, to
   15–18° pitch, then follow the flight director.
9. On **positive climb**, select the landing gear **UP**.
10. Confirm the gear is up on the annunciators and the
    [ECAM WHEEL page](controls.md#system-pages).

> [!NOTE]
> V~1~ is a cyan `1` on the speed tape, V~R~ a cyan circle, V~2~ a magenta
> triangle. On a long runway V~1~ and V~R~ sit so close together that the marks
> overlap. SRS holds V~2~ + 10.

## Initial Climb

Thrust stays at FLX/MCT or TOGA until the thrust reduction altitude — typically
about 1500 ft AGL, and set on the MCDU.

| Control | Action | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [THRUST](controls.md#thrust-and-trim) levers | pull back to the **CL** detent | **LVR CLB** flashing on the FMA |
| [AP 1 / AP 2](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) pushbutton | press **AP1** | after the levers are in CL |
| [FLAPS](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | retract one step | at **S** speed with a positive trend |
| [NOSE](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switch | set **OFF** | after the gear is up |
| [RWY TURN OFF](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switch | set **OFF** | after the gear is up |
| [SPEED BRAKE](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | set **RET**, disarmed | – |
| [PACK 1 & 2](controls.md#air-conditioning-and-pressurisation) pushbuttons | set **ON** | if they were off for takeoff |
| [BARO selector](controls.md#efis-control-panel) | push for **STD** | passing the transition altitude |

> [!NOTE]
> Pulling the levers into CL activates autothrust — the FMA shows `A/THR` in
> white. In normal operation you will not touch the thrust levers again until
> the landing flare.

> [!NOTE]
> Flap retraction depends on the takeoff configuration. From **1+F**, retract at
> **S**. From **2**, retract to 1 at **F** and to 0 at **S**. From **3**, go
> straight to 1+F at **F**.

> [!NOTE]
> The taxi and runway turn-off lights switch themselves off when the gear
> retracts. Move the switches anyway — if the automatic cut-out failed, the
> lamps are now sitting inside the gear bay heating up.

> [!NOTE]
> flyPad: perform the **AFTER TAKEOFF** checklist — gear up, packs on, flaps
> retracted, baro set.

## Climb

Check in with Departure and take the next cleared altitude.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ALT](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) knob | dial the **cleared altitude** | – |
| [ALT](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) knob | **push** for managed CLB, **pull** for OP CLB | – |
| [FMA](controls.md#displays) | verify **THR CLB · CLB · NAV**, ALT armed | – |

> [!NOTE]
> Avoid V/S for climb and descent. V/S guidance outranks speed guidance, so an
> unachievable rate makes the aircraft trade speed for it until a protection
> steps in.

### Passing 10,000 ft

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LAND L & R](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switches | set both **OFF** and retract | – |
| [SEAT BELTS](controls.md#signs) sign | set **OFF** | once stable — no turns, no weather |

The aircraft accelerates to the climb speed on the `PERF CLB` page. Repeat the
climb steps until the cruise level.

## Cruise

The quietest part of the flight: walk the ECAM pages, and expect frequency
changes and position check-ins.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ALT](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) knob | dial the new level and **push** | on a level change |
| [HDG / TRK](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) knob | dial the heading and **pull** | on a vectored heading; push to return to the route |
| [DIR TO](controls.md#flight-plan) page | select the waypoint, press **DIRECT\*** | on a direct-to |
| [SPD / MACH](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) knob | **pull**, then dial the speed | when ATC assigns a speed |

Start descent planning **200–300 NM** out.
