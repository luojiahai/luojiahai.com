# 7 · Approach and ILS Landing

Localizer intercept to runway vacated.

## Intercepting the ILS Localizer

Either inside the instrument approach at the chart altitude and speed, or on
vectors from Approach. Their last instruction is usually to call Tower once
established.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [RAD NAV (LS / FREQ)](controls.md#radio-navigation) page | verify the **ILS frequency** | against the approach chart |
| [LS](controls.md#efis-control-panel) pushbutton | set **ON** | if not already on from 10,000 ft |
| [ND mode](controls.md#efis-control-panel) selector | set **ROSE LS** | optional; shows deviation from the localizer path |
| Intercept | be at the **platform altitude**, angle under **30°** | – |
| [APPROACH](controls.md#performance) page | verify the **APPR phase** is active | usually automatic by now |
| [SPD / MACH](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) knob | **push** for managed speed | decelerates to green dot |
| [FLAPS](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | set **1** | at about V~FE~ − 15, never above V~FE~ |
| [APPR](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) pushbutton | press to **arm LOC and G/S** | – |
| [FMA](controls.md#displays) | verify **LOC** blue, then **green** | established |
| ATC | report **established** on the localizer | to Tower |

> [!NOTE]
> `APPR` arms the glideslope as well, so the aircraft will start down the moment
> it captures. Do not descend without a clearance. To split the two, press
> **LOC** first for the localizer alone and **APPR** once cleared for the
> approach.

> [!NOTE]
> In APPR phase with managed speed, the aircraft targets S speed after FLAPS 1,
> F speed after FLAPS 2, and V~app~ once the landing flap is selected. Aim to be
> in CONF 1 at S speed by the time FLAPS 2 is due.

## Intercepting the ILS Glideslope

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [APPR](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) pushbutton | verify **armed** | **G/S** blue on the FMA |
| [FLAPS](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | set **2** | check the speed against V~FE~ first |
| [FMA](controls.md#displays) | verify **G/S** green | deviation marker centred, descent begins |

> [!NOTE]
> Set FLAPS 2 before the glideslope descent starts — slowing down on the slope
> is hard, and the flap drag is what keeps you from dropping the gear early just
> to lose speed. Airbus guidance puts FLAPS 2 at about 2000 ft on the slope,
> which only works if the speed is already down.

> [!NOTE]
> If ATC assigns an approach speed ("160 kt to 5 miles"), pull for selected
> speed until that point, then push back to managed and take the gear, FLAPS 3
> and FLAPS FULL from there.

> [!NOTE]
> The radio altimeter comes alive at 2500 ft above the ground.

## Preparing for Landing

Fully configured and **stable by 1000 ft** above the ground.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ALT](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) knob | set the **go-around altitude** | from the missed approach procedure |
| [LANDING GEAR](controls.md#autobrake-and-gear) lever | select **DOWN** | 2500–1500 ft, about 5–6 NM out |
| [RWY TURN OFF](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switch | set **ON** | – |
| [NOSE](controls.md#exterior-lighting) switch | set **T.O.** | – |
| [FLAPS](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | set **3**, then **FULL** | check the speed before each |
| [AUTO/BRK](controls.md#autobrake-and-gear) pushbutton | set **LO**, or **MED** | MED in rain, snow or on a short runway |
| [SPEED BRAKE](controls.md#flaps-and-speed-brake) lever | pull up to **ARM** | must be retracted to arm |
| [CALLS ALL](controls.md#calls) pushbutton | press to get **CABIN READY** | – |

> [!NOTE]
> flyPad: perform the **LANDING** checklist.

> [!NOTE]
> FLAPS FULL brings the target down to V~app~. Keep a hand on the thrust levers
> from here for a possible go-around, but do not move them until the last
> seconds.

## Landing

Landing clearance is required — without it, go around.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ATC | obtain the **landing clearance** | – |
| [AP 1 / AP 2](controls.md#flight-control-unit-fcu) pushbutton | press **AP1** off | about 500 ft; leave **A/THR** on |
| PAPI | fly **two white, two red** | correct gently — very little stick is needed |
| Touchdown zone | aim for the **markers and the centre line** | – |

1. Over the threshold at about **50 ft**, look to the far end of the runway.
2. At about **30 ft**, flare — a few degrees of positive pitch, then hold it.
3. At **10–20 ft**, thrust levers to **IDLE**, on the "retard" callout, so you
   are at idle before touchdown.
4. Hold the attitude and let it settle. **Never push the nose down after the
   flare.**
5. On main gear touchdown the speed brakes deploy on their own. Select
   **reverse**.
6. Let the nose gear settle gently and hold the centre line.

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ECAM WHEEL page](controls.md#system-pages) | verify the **spoilers deployed** and autobrake active | – |
| [THRUST](controls.md#thrust-and-trim) levers | out of reverse to **IDLE** | at about 60 kt |
| Brakes | brake manually, **releasing the autobrake** | at about 40 kt |

> [!NOTE]
> For maximum manual braking: select autobrake **MED**, select **MAX reverse**
> at touchdown, then override the autobrake with full pedal deflection as the
> nosewheel touches.

## Vacating the Runway

| Control | Action | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next exit | slow to about **15 kt** before turning off | – |
| Runway holding position | roll clear by the **full length** of the aircraft | – |
| ATC | report **runway vacated** | they will pass taxi instructions |

Flying alone, it is perfectly acceptable to stop clear of the runway and work
through the after-landing flow before moving again.
